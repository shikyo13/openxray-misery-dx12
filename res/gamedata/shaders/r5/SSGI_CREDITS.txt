Native DX12 screen-space indirect lighting

The light-gathering approach was informed by Ascii1457's Screen Space Shaders
indirect-light module, Update14.5, as available in viventaje/xray-16 commit
53639afffa6ff892272a7893ee91250099696e29 (screenspace_il.h).
Author and reuse permission:
https://www.moddb.com/mods/stalker-anomaly/addons/screen-space-shaders

This is a native compute implementation for the fork's world normals, reverse-Z
depth, material colors and authored fog. It is not the newer SSS23 implementation.
It adds emitter/receiver orientation, short visibility checks, fog removal,
ambient exclusion and depth/normal-aware filtering. Only visible surfaces can
contribute; ordinary ambient lighting remains the fallback outside that coverage.
